# KA Wedding — Privacy Policy (draft)

_Draft for Kashika & Arjun to review before publishing. Replace the contact line._

KA Wedding is a private app for guests and vendors of Kashika & Arjun's wedding
(17–19 January 2027). You need an invite code to use it.

**What we store**

- The name you type when you join.
- Photos you take with the in-app camera, and messages, photos, voice notes, reactions and quiz answers you post.
- Travel documents (an ID and tickets), only if you choose to add them, so the wedding team can arrange travel and check-in.
- Your RSVP: whether you're coming, how many people and their names, any food needs, a note, and a phone number if you give one.
- An optional introduction other guests can see: a photo, which side you're on, your city and a line about how you know the couple.
- A notification token for your phone, if you turn notifications on.
- SOS alerts you send, with your name and any note you add.

**What we do not collect**

- Your location. The optional arrival welcome is handled entirely by your phone; your location is never sent to us or anyone else.
- Your contacts, email or payment details. A phone number is stored only if you type one into your RSVP.

**Who can see what**

- RSVP replies and phone numbers are seen only by the couple and their wedding planner.
- Room numbers: you see only your own. Sangeet tables: guests can see the seating chart.
- Guests see guest chat rooms, the shared album and each other's introductions. Vendors see only their own team's room. The couple and their wedding planner can see everything and can remove content.
- Album and chat photos and voice notes are stored at unguessable links that the app shows only to people allowed to see them.
- Travel documents are different: they sit in a private vault. Only you and the couple's team can open them, through links that stop working after two minutes, and each opening is logged. Other guests and vendors can never see them.

**Your choices**

- Report or block: hold any message, or open any photo.
- Delete: You → "Delete my account and everything I posted" removes your name, messages, photos, voice notes, travel documents, reactions and quiz answers straight away. You can also delete any single travel document at any time.

**Where it is stored**

Supabase (database and file storage). Notifications are delivered through Expo, Apple and Google.

**How long**

Travel documents are erased by the couple's team once travel is complete, and no later than [date — suggest 31 January 2027]. We plan to delete all other app data by [date — suggest 30 April 2027], after guests have had time to save their photos.
